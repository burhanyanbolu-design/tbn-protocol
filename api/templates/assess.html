Why This Assessment Exists

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) is now in force. High-risk AI systems must demonstrate governance, traceability, human oversight, and compliance by August 2026. Most organisations deploying AI agents have no infrastructure to prove any of this. This free assessment helps you identify exactly where your gaps are before enforcement begins.

What Is AI Governance Readiness?

AI governance readiness measures how prepared your organisation is to deploy AI agents safely, compliantly, and accountably. It covers agent identity verification, authority boundaries, runtime enforcement, audit trails, EU AI Act compliance, data governance, security monitoring, and emergency override processes. Organisations with low readiness scores face regulatory fines, security breaches, and operational failures when AI agents act outside their boundaries.

EU AI Act Enforcement Timeline

The EU AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024. Key enforcement dates that affect AI agent deployments:

  • February 2025 — Prohibited AI practices banned
  • August 2025 — GPAI model obligations apply
  • August 2026 — High-risk AI system requirements enforced (Articles 9, 13, 14, 15, 26)
  • August 2027 — Full enforcement including Annex I systems

Organisations deploying AI agents in healthcare, finance, legal, employment, education, or law enforcement are classified as high-risk under Annex III and must demonstrate full compliance by August 2026.
