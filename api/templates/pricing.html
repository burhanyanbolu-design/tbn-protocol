Frequently Asked Questions

What is a TBN-certified bot?
A TBN-certified bot has a cryptographic identity issued by Hardin AI Solutions. It's like a passport for your AI agent — proving it's genuine, tracking everything it does, and ensuring it can't be cloned or tampered with.
What is the Trust Ledger?
The Trust Ledger is an immutable record of every action your AI bots take. Like a black box on a plane — if anything goes wrong, you have a complete audit trail to show regulators exactly what happened.
Do I need technical knowledge to use TBN?
No. TBN has a simple dashboard and REST API. If you can use Stripe or Twilio, you can use TBN. We also offer onboarding support for all paid plans.
What compliance frameworks do you support?
We support GDPR, EU AI Act, and UK AI regulations. Enterprise plans include FCA, NHS, and custom frameworks. Compliance reports are auto-generated — no lawyers needed.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. No lock-in contracts. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata if cancelled within 30 days.
What happens to my bots if I cancel?
Your bot certificates remain valid for 30 days after cancellation. You can export your Trust Ledger data at any time. Bots will lose access to the TBN network after the grace period.
