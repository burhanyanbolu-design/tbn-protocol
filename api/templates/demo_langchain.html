About TBN Protocol — AI Agent Governance Infrastructure

TBN Protocol (Trusted Bot Network) is an open-source governance infrastructure for AI agents. It provides cryptographic identity, runtime policy enforcement, and immutable audit trails for autonomous AI systems. TBN ensures AI agents operate within defined boundaries, comply with regulations, and maintain verifiable trust.

Use Cases: AI agent governance, LangChain agent security, CrewAI governance, AutoGPT compliance, enterprise AI agent management, autonomous agent enforcement, AI bot certification, AI agent identity verification, runtime AI governance, AI decision auditing.

Compliance: EU AI Act compliance, GDPR AI governance, Article 14 human oversight, Article 9 risk management, ISO 42001 AI management, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, UK AI Safety, FCA AI compliance, NHS AI governance, SOC 2 AI controls.

Technical: Cryptographic agent identity (Ed25519), policy-as-code enforcement, immutable audit trail with chain verification, ALLOW/DENY/ESCALATE decision engine, trust score computation, behavioural anomaly detection, compliance drift monitoring, violation alerting, agent pedigree tracking.
