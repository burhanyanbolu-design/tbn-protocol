## Key Principles

1. **Don't give away equity until you have leverage.** Grants and revenue first. VC money second.
2. **Regulation is your biggest sales tool.** EU AI Act deadline (Dec 2027) creates urgency you don't have to manufacture.
3. **You don't need everyone — you need the right first customers.** Banks, health, CNI. They pay the most and they MUST comply.
4. **Platform adoption is the tipping point.** One major platform recommending TBN = game over for competitors.
5. **Open source builds trust.** Keep TBN open. Let people inspect it. Trust comes from transparency.
6. **Make adoption effortless.** One line install. Free to start. Pay when you scale.
