# Personal Plan — Burhan Yanbolu

## Building TBN While Living Well

**Age:** 53  
**Horizon:** 3–7 years building, 30+ years enjoying  
**Goal:** Build something valuable, protect health, secure family, exit or step back with financial freedom  

---

## What I Want

1. Build TBN into something valuable (3–7 years)
2. Enjoy life and health DURING the build (not sacrifice everything)
3. Exit or step back around 56–60
4. Live well for 30+ years after
5. Train my family to manage the wealth/assets

---

## The Two Paths

| Path | How it works | Risk | Reward |
|------|-------------|------|--------|
| **Build and sell** | Build TBN for 3–5 years, exit, live off proceeds | All-or-nothing if exit fails | Clean break, lump sum |
| **Build and hold** | Build TBN, take dividends, hire a CEO to run it, step back | Slower wealth, more responsibility | Ongoing income, family legacy |

**Best approach: Hybrid.** Build it, get it to a point where it runs without me (hire a CEO), then either sell OR keep it as a family asset that pays dividends.

---

## How to Protect Myself During the Build

| Priority | Action | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Health** | Don't work 16-hour days. Set boundaries. Exercise. | Dead founders don't exit. |
| **Income** | Pay myself from grants/revenue as soon as possible | Can't build well if stressed about rent |
| **Family** | Bring them in early — even in small roles | They learn the business, I'm not alone |
| **Insurance** | Key-man insurance, life insurance | Protects family if something happens to me |
| **Structure** | Keep shares in a family trust or holding company | Tax efficient, protects assets |
